# Teams

Gen 1 OU teams in **Pokémon Showdown export format** (the text you get from the teambuilder's
"Import/Export" button). Gen 1 has no abilities, items, natures, or EV/IV spreads to specify,
so each set is just a species name followed by up to four moves.

- `gen1ou-sample.txt` — a standard Gen 1 OU team (the classic Tauros / Snorlax / Chansey /
  Exeggutor / Starmie / Alakazam core). Good as a fixed team for early training and for
  sanity-checking the pipeline.

For training, start with one or a few **fixed teams** here and treat team-building as a
separate problem to tackle later (see `docs/roadmap.md`).
